01 · OUR PROMISE

Zero collection. Zero internet access.

Luma does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Production Android builds do not request android.permission.INTERNET. Financial records, categories, subscriptions, settings, and parsed SMS data live exclusively in an on-device SQLite database managed by Drift. They never leave your phone.

SMS parsing happens 100% locally on your device. Luma has no cloud sync, analytics service, or account system.

02 · PERMISSIONS

What the app accesses

  • On-device only
    RECEIVE_SMS

    Receives incoming SMS broadcasts so supported bank and UPI transaction messages can be detected.

  • On-device only
    READ_SMS

    Lets you scan recent messages when you choose to import transaction history. Parsing stays on device.

  • On-device only
    POST_NOTIFICATIONS

    Shows reminders and locally detected transaction notifications on Android versions that require permission.

  • On-device only
    RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

    Restores scheduled local reminders after your phone restarts.

03 · YOUR CONTROL

Delete your data

In Luma, open Settings → Delete All Records → Reset everything, type confirm, then tap Confirm reset. This removes your local app records and settings. Uninstalling Luma also removes its app data from your device.
